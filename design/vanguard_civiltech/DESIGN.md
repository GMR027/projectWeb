## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for mission-critical civil engineering, spatial infrastructure, and industrial asset intelligence. The core brand personality balances sovereign structural authority with high-precision digital telemetry. The aesthetic merges **Dark Precision Engineering** with **Refined Industrial Glassmorphism**: deep structural navy and atmospheric dark slates provide a stable bedrock, while optical-grade transparent overlays and laser-sharp industrial safety accents guide decisive operator actions.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Primary Audience:** Infrastructure directors, lead structural engineers, geospatial analysts, and construction site intelligence managers.
- **Cognitive & Emotional Response:** Absolute confidence, operational clarity under cognitive load, rigorous technical excellence, and the tactility of high-spec avionics and industrial telemetry hardware.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-refined dark working environment optimized for multi-screen operations, structural digital twins, and sensor feed readability.

### Core Functional Assignments
- **Primary Industrial Signal (`#FF5A1F`):** High-visibility safety orange reserved strictly for high-impact interactions, critical primary executions, alarm triggers, and structural alert statuses.
- **Secondary Telemetry Blue (`#00B4D8`):** Electromagnetic laser-cyan designating real-time data streams, live sensor signals, vectors, active structural nodes, and running simulations.
- **Tertiary Structural Slate (`#3A506B`):** Supporting technical boundaries, inactive geometric coordinate plots, secondary metadata, and structural wireframe elements.
- **Neutral Deep Trench (`#0B132B`):** Deep foundation canvas that reduces ambient eye strain while maximizing the dynamic range of glowing telemetry.
- **Sub-Surface Canvas (`#1C2541`):** Mid-depth structural panels, elevated telemetry trays, and secondary surface layers.

### Surface Color Logic
- **Base Environment:** `#0B132B`
- **Raised Glass Plate:** `rgba(28, 37, 65, 0.65)` layered with backdrop blur.
- **Floating Overlays & Tooltips:** `rgba(58, 80, 107, 0.45)` with subtle surface illumination.
- **Borders & Dividers:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` to `rgba(0, 180, 216, 0.2)`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements an engineering-grade tri-font architecture designed to separate structural positioning, human reading flow, and numeric data readout.

### Typographic Roles
- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Provides a geometric, architectural, and forward-looking frame for dashboard titles, structural indices, and major section partitions.
- **Narrative & UI Body (`Manrope`):** Offers balanced grotesque neutrality with open letterforms, ensuring that analytical logs and multi-line reports remain legible in low-contrast environments.
- **Technical Readouts & Telemetry (`JetBrains Mono`):** Dedicated to coordinates, structural loads, sensor values, tolerances, and timeline timestamps. Monospaced tabular alignment ensures zero visual shifting during rapid numerical updates.

## Layout & Spacing

Complex industrial data systems often suffer from cognitive overload. This system counters that failure mode with a spacious, modular layout model rooted in deliberate negative space and disciplined grid alignment.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Responsive Modular Fluid Grid:** 12-column grid system on desktop (`min-width: 1280px`), 8 columns on tablet (`768px` to `1279px`), and 4 columns on mobile viewports (`<768px`).
- **Telemetry Trays:** Lateral contextual panels (BIM viewers, sensor arrays) remain pinned or collapse smoothly without reflowing primary viewport geometry.
- **Spatial Rhythms:** Internal card padding is locked to `space-lg` (`24px`) to preserve structural detachment between dense numeric tables and vector viewports. Micro-spacings (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) are strictly enforced for inline tags, metrics, and associated coordinate pairs.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through tempered glassmorphism, directional rim lighting, and structural layered tiers rather than standard diffuse drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Foundation Bedrock):** Raw canvas `#0B132B`. Deep, non-reflective backdrop for high-resolution graphics and 3D terrain rendering.
- **Tier 1 (Tempered Glass Deck):** Background `rgba(28, 37, 65, 0.65)` with `16px` backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), framed by a crisp edge line: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Modules & Popovers):** Background `rgba(36, 48, 80, 0.85)` with `24px` backdrop blur, a directional top highlight `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`, and a subterranean contact shadow: `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(3, 7, 18, 0.65)`.
- **Tier 3 (Active Telemetry Focus):** Overlays displaying active sensor warnings or calibration tools gain a refined laser aura: `0 0 24px -4px rgba(0, 180, 216, 0.25)` or safety emergency luminescence: `0 0 28px -4px rgba(255, 90, 31, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a restrained **Soft (`1`)** shape language (`4px` base radius, `8px` container radius, `12px` modal radius). In heavy industrial and civil engineering contexts, excessive roundedness diminishes structural authority.

- **Interactive Nodes & Triggers:** Base components use `4px` to mirror milled composite panels.
- **Cards, Trays & Viewports:** Containers use `8px` (`rounded-lg`) to maintain crisp technical framing without visual harshness.
- **Modal Dialogs & Floating HUDs:** Floating contextual overlays use `12px` (`rounded-xl`).
- **Telemetry Indicators:** Pills and badges retain a sharp structural silhouette rather than organic forms.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Industrial Action:** Vibrant Safety Orange background (`#FF5A1F`), stark high-contrast typography, `4px` corner radius. Hover state: fine edge glow with slight luminance step-up (`#FF6E38`). Active state: micro-compression scale `0.98`.
- **Secondary Telemetry Action:** Frosted slate background `rgba(58, 80, 107, 0.4)` with a hairline boundary `1px solid rgba(0, 180, 216, 0.4)`. Text rendered in electromagnetic cyan (`#00B4D8`).
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** No background fill, `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text in muted cool white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Fields:** Tempered dark surface (`rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.8)`), inset hairline border (`1px solid rgba(58, 80, 107, 0.5)`), label styled using `JetBrains Mono` sm. Focus state: crisp cyan border (`#00B4D8`) with ambient inward cyan highlight.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Precision-machined appearance. Inactive: hollow slate borders. Active: solid cyan or safety orange fill with an inner mechanical check indicator.

### Chips & Telemetry Badges
- **Format:** Micro-padding (`2px 8px`), monospaced font (`JetBrains Mono`), semi-translucent background (`rgba(0, 180, 216, 0.12)`), paired with a pulsing live status dot (`6px` diameter).
- **Alarm Variant:** Background `rgba(255, 90, 31, 0.14)`, text `#FF5A1F`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 90, 31, 0.3)`.

### Cards & Module Containers
- **Construction:** Structured as tempered glass sheets (`rgba(28, 37, 65, 0.65)` with backdrop blur). Headers incorporate technical hairline separators (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`). Content blocks remain uncrowded, relying on `space-lg` padding to isolate metrics from vector renderings.

### CivilTech-Specific Components
- **Geotechnical Metric Strip:** Monospaced tabular strip displaying live coordinate shifts, seismic tolerances, and structural stress loads with color-coded drift indicators.
- **Spatial Viewport HUD:** Floating glass overlays positioned over 3D BIM or topological maps, complete with coordinate grid marks, zoom scale factors, and layer visibility toggles.
